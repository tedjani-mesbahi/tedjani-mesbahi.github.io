## Research themes

1. Battery modelling and digital twins (electro-thermal, ageing, Na-ion, parameter identification).
2. Hybrid energy storage and energy management (battery / supercapacitor / fuel cell; EVs, forklifts, long-endurance UAVs).
3. Explainable AI for battery management (SOC, SOH, RUL; LSTM, PINN, spiking neural networks, counterfactuals, ontologies).
4. Battery safety and thermal runaway (fire effluents, prevention, partnership with SIS 67).
5. Rare-earth-free electric drives (synchronous reluctance machines).
6. Sustainable and traceable batteries (digital battery passport, battery production ontology, second life, TCO).
